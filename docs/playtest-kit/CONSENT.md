# Before you play

Thank you for helping. Here is what this session involves.

**What you'll do.** Play *CISO: First Year*, a game about running cyber
security for a company for one year, for about half an hour. There are no
right answers. We are testing the game, not you.

**What we'll note.** The facilitator will take notes on what you look at, what
you say aloud and the questions you ask. At the end we will ask you a few
questions about the year you played.

**What the game records.** With your agreement, the game keeps a record on
this device of which screens you opened and which choices you made, with the
in-game date and the time. It does not record anything you type, your name, or
anything outside the game. Nothing is sent over the internet: at the end, the
file is saved on this device and handed to the facilitator. You can see that
recording is on — a red dot at the bottom of the menu — and you can switch it
off at any point without giving a reason.

**How it will be used.** Your notes and the file are used only to improve the
game. Anything written up about the session describes what happened, not who
you are. You will not be named.

**You can stop at any time**, and ask for your notes and file to be deleted
afterwards, without giving a reason.
