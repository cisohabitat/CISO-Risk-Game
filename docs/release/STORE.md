Text for a games storefront that accepts browser games (for example
itch.io), kept here so every listing says the same thing. Publishing a
listing is the owner's decision; nothing here is posted anywhere.

**Title:** CISO: First Year

**Short description (under 140 characters):**
You are the new head of cyber security. You cannot fix everything. Decide
what matters, and live with the year that follows.

**Long description:**

You have just been made Chief Information Security Officer of Nexora Group.
You inherit 6,283 critical vulnerabilities, a risk register nobody trusts and
a CEO who wants three answers in twenty-three minutes.

*CISO: First Year* is a single-player strategy game about the judgement of a
security leader. Spend a budget that does not refill and a week of attention
you cannot stretch. Find out which parts of what you inherited are true.
Persuade executives who want different things. Decide what the board hears.
Then meet the incident that tests it all — or the quiet year that tests
whether you looked.

- A full year in twenty to forty minutes, on phone or desktop
- Three modes and four starting situations
- No score: an annual review that reads your year in eight dimensions
- Share a year, or give a class the same organisation with a link
- No account, nothing to install; free

singleplayer, browser
