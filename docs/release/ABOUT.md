# CISO: First Year

**You have just been made Chief Information Security Officer of Nexora Group.**
You inherit 6,283 critical vulnerabilities, a risk register nobody trusts, four
transformation programmes and a CEO who wants three answers in twenty-three
minutes. You cannot fix everything. The job is to work out what actually
matters, decide what deserves your attention, influence people you do not
control, and live with the consequences.

*CISO: First Year* is a single-player strategy game about the judgement of a
security leader, played in the browser over one simulated year.

## What it is

- **A year in twenty to forty minutes.** The clock runs and stops itself for
  anything that needs you: a decision falling due, an incident, the quarter's
  board paper.
- **Two scarce things.** A budget that does not refill, and five actions a
  week of your own attention. Everything competes for them.
- **A picture that is partly wrong.** What you inherit is somebody else's
  word. Commissioning the work that checks it is how you find out what is
  true — and what you never check stays hidden.
- **People with their own priorities.** Eight executives and four cyber
  leaders remember how you treated them. The board hears what you choose to
  tell it.
- **No score.** The year closes on an annual review: eight dimensions, each a
  band and a sentence, and an incident reconstruction if one came. It can be
  printed, or shared with a link that opens the same year for whoever
  receives it.

## Who it is for

Anyone curious about what a security leader actually does — no technical
knowledge needed; the game explains its words where they appear. Practising
security people tend to recognise their own year in it. It is also built to be
taught with: a facilitator's pack sets out a ninety-minute session, questions
for each quarter, and seeds that give a whole class the same organisation.

## Feedback and problems

Report a problem or suggest something on the
[project's issue tracker](https://github.com/cisohabitat/CISO-Risk-Game/issues).
Say which mode you were playing, roughly where in the year, and on what
device; the seed (shown on **Your year**) lets anyone replay the same world.

## Known limitations

- **Saves live on the device you play on.** Use **Export this campaign** on
  **Your year** to move a campaign to another device or keep it safe.
- **It needs a connection to start.** It does not yet work offline.
- **English only**, and one organisation, Nexora, so far.
- **Not yet played with a screen reader by someone who uses one**; see the
  [accessibility statement](/accessibility).

Nexora Group is fictional. Any resemblance to your own organisation is a
coincidence you should probably act on.
