# Content flow audit — 20 October 2026

## 21 October addition: AI-accelerated checkout exploit

The owner authorised a focused expansion of the existing exploit decision.
The same six-seed command was rerun with that content: 24 distinct campaign
years and 24 deterministic repeats pass daily invariants. Current inventory is
222 events, 49 decisions, 149 options and 74 scheduled reply targets, with no
unreferenced replies or exact duplicate authored bodies. All six paired policies
still differ in choices, received callbacks and review records. Figures below
remain the original 20 October measurements rather than a new pacing claim.

Focused engine tests cover every option's response, the live logging-coverage
branch, capacity release, scheduled patching and late-year save/transition.
`pnpm soak 30 --years 2` also completed 30 two-year campaigns across the three
modes with no crashes or invariant failures. None of these probes establishes
human understanding, engagement or attribution of AI use against Nexora.

Scope: promises and follow-through, repeated mail, quiet-period visibility and
replay consistency. Base revision: `114d237`; measurements include this
changeset's fixes. No human sessions were run for this audit.

## Reproduce

```bash
pnpm --silent audit:content-flow 6 > /tmp/ciso-content-flow.json
```

Seeds `content-audit-1` through `content-audit-6` use fixed CISO mode and the
inherited-mess opening. Two policies use first/reverse affordable options and
identity/cloud programme orders. Both attempt two enquiries per year, submit
material board papers, resolve affordable blockers and clear mail daily. Each
starts at most one new programme per year and does not resume paused programmes.
These are probes, not optimal players or isolated single-choice experiments.

Each policy plays two years and is replayed exactly: **24 distinct campaign
years plus 24 repeated years**. Every simulated day checks invariants. The
command asserts matching starting worlds and deterministic repeats, and prints
source mappings, choices, callback IDs, programme states, review bands and
duplicate counts. A log is not evidence of reading or understanding.

## Promises and follow-through

Inventory: **217 events, 49 decisions, 148 options, 69 scheduled-event targets
and 14 repeatable events**. Every scheduled-only event has an authored scheduler;
every scheduled target resolves. No authored message bodies are exact duplicates.
This does not prove that every natural-language promise is implemented.

| Major trace | Recorded response |
|---|---|
| All six opening routes | Route-specific callback after 95 days |
| CEO framing | Honest/confident replies at 110 days; deferral at 90 |
| Launch support, conditions, opposition | Distinct replies at 70 days; compensation has a 60-day fix and an 80-day request for an account |
| Recruitment and audit commitment | Recruitment callback at 55 days; audit dates requested at 100 |
| Q4 audit claim | At 30 days, different replies according to actual identity programme completion |
| Q4 priorities | Replies at 20 days; risk-led priorities distinguish whether any board papers were submitted |
| Corvus, Kestrel and second-year choices | Option-specific replies; see the full source mapping in the JSON |

**Confirmed defect:** annual transition kept scheduled callbacks but cleared
their guard flags. Regression tests reproduced a late priorities reply and a
carried audit response disappearing; both failed before the fix.

Pending entries now carry only their needed story flags. Capability and
suppression are checked at delivery. Facts stay local to the reply: a carried
budget callback cannot become another autumn budget decision for year three.
Another reproduced defect checked January's empty paper list when judging a
previous-year priorities commitment. Historical paper guards now use that
commitment's year record, including within nested conditions.
The estate retirement date keeps its normal shift. Older saves whose facts were
already discarded are not repaired by guessing.

## Repeated mail

occurrence per year were:

Duplicates require identical sender, subject and body; counts exclude the 24
replays. Identical incident-command text can concern different incidents, and
repeatable threats can still carry effects. No messages are consolidated or
suppressed from counts alone. These subjects are concrete human-test candidates.

## Quiet-period visibility

The Briefing showed programme percentage/confidence without naming paused status.
It now names the recorded status and next outstanding milestone. Enquiry names
wrap, with approximate return dates instead of a bare day count. Detailed costs,
blockers and actions remain in their workspaces; hidden schedules stay hidden.
The programme empty state no longer claims that nothing can improve outside a
funded programme: decisions can also address specific weaknesses.

Longest gap between decision arrival dates, including the closing tail:

| Policy | Year | Six-seed range | Median |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Identity, first affordable options | 1 | 64–69 days | 69 |
| Identity, first affordable options | 2 | 82–135 days | 112 |
| Cloud, reverse affordable options | 1 | 73–84 days | 84 |
| Cloud, reverse affordable options | 2 | 81–163 days | 160 |

These are decision-arrival gaps, not periods without messages, incidents,
enquiry returns or programme work. Policy and branch coverage matter. Observe
these stretches with players before adding events or changing pacing.

## Replay consistency

All six seed pairs started from the same respective world. All same-policy
replays matched. All contrasting-policy pairs differed in choices, sets of
received follow-up events and annual review records. This establishes branch
sensitivity, not whether people notice variety or want to replay.

The next-year builder is deferred until requested to preserve the existing
download budget. Its old orchestrator re-export made it a static dependency;
engine harnesses now import it directly. Browser regressions cover repeated
clicks creating one year and failed loading preserving the finished campaign
and export route. The builder stays pure and outside the initial chunk list.
