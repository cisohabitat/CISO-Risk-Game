# Five-run CISO follow-through — 30 October 2026

[The supplied account](playtests/2026-10-30-owner-supplied-five-run-ciso-review.md)
is one account of five differently played/configured campaigns. Builds, seeds,
exports and independence are unknown. These findings test current source and
controlled fixtures; they do not reconstruct those campaigns or establish
perceived fairness, engagement or learning.

## Reproduced rules and changes

- **December:** the earlier correspondence handover made final mail readable,
  but the last tick still finished the campaign before a newly opened final-day
  decision could be answered. UI advances now request a held final tick. Day 364
  remains playable, saveable and frozen in time until Close year. Unanswered
  choices require explicit acknowledgement before their authored defaults run.
  Closing adds no day or weekly attention. Ordinary headless ticks/runDays still
  finish automatically; old finished saves retain their routing and grades.
  Explicitly requested early write-up remains available; only automatic closure
  waits for the player at the final date.
  UI skips quote seven days, stop for notable updates and all December mail.
  Quiet weeks remain possible; no filler decisions or fake activity are added.
- **Business results:** a weak business dimension previously permitted Credible
  while weak prioritisation/team health imposed Mixed. Weak business enablement
  now shares that critical ceiling. The eight equal weights, other ceilings and
  stored annual reviews are preserved. This is a minimum standard, not a new
  weighted score or a difficulty-dependent grader.
- **Objective dates:** the engine checked achievement before expiry, permitting
  one extra progress tick after a commitment. Expiry now precedes that tick.
  The live tracker shows each public commitment, owner, progress, status,
  recorded delay and security support. It never exposes undiscovered dependency
  health. A recorded 30-day progress penalty can fit inside existing margin;
  it is not necessarily a 30-day observed late delivery. Original dates stay fixed.
  The exact reported Nordic failures remain unidentified.
- **Burnout:** `functionStrain` returned full strain at zero capacity even with
  no work; the vacancy penalty also applied while idle. Empty functions now have
  zero strain; vacancy pressure scales with actual strain. Idle recovery and
  sustained-overload fixtures pass without changing their daily rate constants.
  Recovery remains gradual. Wellbeing names actual workload/capacity, vacancies
  and direction, so a purchased improvement can show recovery before its band
  crosses. Capacity does not guarantee an instantly healthy team. Navigation
  keeps its intrinsic height while the expanded resource details scroll in the
  remaining rail space; short windows retain their whole-rail fallback.
- **Recruitment:** the £140k choice promises one identity hire after 60 days,
  not filling all five vacancies. The actual vacancy/capacity mutation is now
  retained in a resource receipt. Team permits recruiting another uncovered
  vacancy while one hire is incoming; the engine refuses duplicate spend when
  all remaining vacancies already have incoming hires. Direct hire arrivals
  also send a retained recruitment update.
- **Mail and pacing:** update badges exclude reference mail and resolved decision
  letters. All/Unread retain every message, with a Reference filter and explicit
  Mark reference mail read. Signals, returns and resource-changing event mail
  remain updates. Recent returns leave Briefing after 14 days; Recent history
  shows seven days. Neither rule deletes correspondence or hides current work.
  Refunds retain their actual ledger entry and Skip stops at the movement.
- **Consequences:** the annual account names observed negative authored returns
  from recorded selections, including the quiet payment-scope fix and an audit
  account contradicted by production. It does not manufacture a penalty if the
  claim was borne out, infer a missing historical consequence, or blame a choice
  alone for an incident. The watch option declares controls it leaves open, so
  existing reconstruction can name it on the actual still-exposed route.
  SOC warning readers explain the targeted hunt and its real commissioning route.
  Reconstruction checks commissioning/return dates rather than saying every
  warning was ignored; a hunt remains distinct from successful eviction.
- **Other reports:** paused timeline spans say paused, including year end.
  Priorities can cite delivered programmes. Assumptions offer public enquiry
  routes without manually revealing hidden truth. Accepted risks remain required
  board disclosures; acceptance does not remove materiality. Previous papers
  are read-only records; only one pending quarter exists. Stopped enquiries keep
  sunk costs, now explained before commissioning. Neither intended rule was
  reversed to clear a reported symptom. Acceptance expiry remains separate from
  an earlier reassessment date; a 90-day expiry fixture pins that distinction.
  Budget reserve guidance is visible alongside current funding quotes.

## Matched probes

`pnpm --silent audit:balance 30` was run at base `400d2a3` and after the changes.
There are 30 common seeds × four declared programme policies × three modes:
360 campaign-years per arm, using the default opening. Each policy attempts one
fully funded programme, two enquiries, first affordable responses with a £350k
reserve, affordable blocker clearance, and material board papers. All 360
programmes started in both arms; neither arm had defaults or invariant failures.
This is a policy comparison: downstream events and decisions can diverge, and
several fixes changed together. It does not isolate a single causal effect.

| Mode | Incidents/year before → after | Objectives achieved / 5 before → after | Weak team dimensions before → after |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Guided | 0.408 → 0.408 | 4.800 → 4.750 | 15/120 → 0/120 |
| CISO | 1.042 → 1.025 | 4.783 → 4.775 | 21/120 → 0/120 |
| High pressure | 1.542 → 1.533 | 4.725 → 4.683 | 17/120 → 0/120 |

Post-change team bands are not universally strong: Developing occurs in
10/120 Guided, 40/120 CISO and 66/120 High pressure years. These policies carry
only one programme and two enquiries, unlike a heavily commissioned human year.
The change removes idle vacancy attrition; it does not show that aggressive
multi-programme delivery is sustainable or close a human balance gate.

`pnpm --silent audit:balance 30 all` then compares all four explicit openings
with the same seeds/policies across modes: 1,440 current campaign-years, no
defaults or invariant failures. Each row below contains 120 years per mode.

| Opening | Mean incidents Guided / CISO / High pressure | Mean objectives Guided / CISO / High pressure |
|---|---|---|
| Inherited mess | 0.408 / 1.025 / 1.533 | 4.750 / 4.775 / 4.683 |
| After the breach | 0.392 / 1.092 / 1.583 | 4.758 / 4.758 / 4.675 |
| New money | 0.292 / 1.025 / 1.392 | 4.750 / 4.767 / 4.683 |
| Tidy inheritance | 0.392 / 0.825 / 1.358 | 4.750 / 4.775 / 4.667 |

Threat outcomes scale in every opening. Difficulty profiles are unchanged:
there is no basis here to increase noise or arbitrarily squeeze resources again.
Overall verdicts are **not monotonic difficulty measures**. Credible occurs in
97/103/106 default-opening years and 101/105/102 New-money years respectively;
the grader rewards managed/testing capability, not simply a lack of incidents.
Further perceived-difficulty and verdict calibration remains a human-review
question. These data do not disprove the supplied player's smooth High pressure
experience or establish that the harder mode feels hard enough.

The objective audit was also rerun: six component cases plus 108 full years,
all intended launch choices recorded, no defaults or invariants. Component
outcomes match the earlier [objective review](OBJECTIVE_REVIEW.md). The full
High-pressure/Oppose cell now achieves the platform in 8/12 years (mean 4.667
objectives), versus the earlier 7/12 (4.58); other cells match to rounding.
Changed workload and later option availability can alter downstream paths, so
this difference is not attributed solely to the deadline correction.
