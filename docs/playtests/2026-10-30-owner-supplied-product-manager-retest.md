## Supplied account

> I replayed the full High pressure year on the same scenario and got a "Mixed first year". It met 3 of 5 objectives, the ones it missed were the platform launch and the Nordic market, and it was capped by weak team sustainability. This run had two incidents, a platform-tampering attack in March and ransomware in May, and I handled every decision. Two small issues from last time are fixed, but most of the bigger problems are still there, and I found a new pacing bug.
>
> Still broken
> - Required "material" board items still start unticked.
> - If you tick everything, the board now names which items it didn't need, which is an improvement. There's still no hint beforehand about how many items is right.
> - The board gives the same reply whenever it approves: "The board follows the argument and supports the direction you set out."
> - There's still no Q4 board paper. The year ends with only three offered.
> - The review still says "38 of 38 systems were ever brought into view" while also listing "8 material dependencies were never discovered".
> - Business enablement is still "solid" with 2 objectives missed.
> - On High pressure the team hits "burning out" by late February whatever you do. Meanwhile all three programmes finished, so delivery feels too easy compared with how harsh the team score is.
>
> Fixed or not seen this time
> - The Q1/Q4 mismatch between the sidebar and the header is gone.
> - The disabled "Lead with the programmes you are running" option was correct this time, because no programme was actually running.
> - The game now says up front that material decisions need a reason.
> - The double full stop didn't come up, because I never triggered that rebuke.
>
> New issues
> - The clock carries on after you submit a paper or spend attention. At 4× a week passes in about 2–3 seconds, so I lost several weeks of attention. Once, about six weeks passed with no pause at all.
> - The Q3 paper never announced itself. It was due around 1 October, but I only found it on 15 November under the Board tab, and the record says it was "Prepared 15 November".
> - Paper deadlines are confusing. The Q1 paper says "Submit by 1 July" and is "presented in Q2".
> - The undiscovered-systems count disagrees between screens. The Briefing said 8 systems were undiscovered while Organisation said 6, possibly at slightly different times.
> - The budget runs out by about May on High pressure. After that, 7 decisions had options out of reach, and the ransomware programme stalled until a £150k fix.
>
> Smaller issues:
> - The review calls the programme "Recovery uplift" when its real name is "Ransomware resilience and recovery".
> - A ransomware incident with "severe consequence" still scored resilience as "developing".
>
> What I'd fix next
> - Stop the clock after any action at high speed, and flag every board paper with its own alert.
> - Give a target range for board items, and vary the board's replies.
> - Reconcile the discovery counts in the review.
> - Ease the burnout curve or tighten programme delivery so the two feel balanced.
>
> Two caveats. Some choices were made quickly rather than thoughtfully, and I let weekly attention lapse after Q1, so those parts are less reliable.
