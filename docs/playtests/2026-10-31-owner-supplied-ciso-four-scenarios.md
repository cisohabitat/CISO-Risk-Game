### What a newcomer learns on CISO

Budget is the real difficulty. Funding the two obvious programmes in January
uses up most of the money. In "A tidy inheritance" I was down to £30k by week 6,
and six later decisions had options greyed out as "more than the £30k left this
year". Attention was usually left unspent.

Business delivery always suffers. Across all four scenarios I met only 0 to 3
of the 5 objectives, and the most common headline was "the business paid for it
in delivery". A newcomer can't see that cost while playing. It only shows up in
the review.

Blind spots scored weak in all four runs. Same as Guided: I examined only 2 to 4
of about 30 things I could have.

Scenario decisions worked well. "Your predecessor's numbers", "Next year's
budget after a quiet year", "The board wants something it can see" and the
extortion refusal all made sense, and the review quoted each one back.

### Bugs found on CISO

- The year-end review can fail to appear. In one run, clicking "End the year
  and write it up" left me on the timeline with "Write up the year now" still
  active. I only got the review after a hard reload and Continue. A newcomer
  would think the game had frozen.
- Board papers can go in empty. With no risks raised, the paper said "No agenda
  topics are eligible yet" and still accepted the submission.
- The review contradicts itself. It said 38 of 38 systems were found but also
  "8 material dependencies never discovered", while the Briefing said 15. It
  also said resilience was strong but "Recovery was never exercised".
- Budget changes with no explanation. The budget went up by £60k to £90k a few
  times with nothing saying why.
- Long silent jumps. Skip ahead jumped from week 22 to 29 and from 4 Aug to
  1 Oct with no stops.
- The quarter recap overlay hides Skip ahead until you dismiss it.
- Small issues: the week counter lags the calendar date, every enquiry was
  dated "8 Jan", and "Record why first" still looks clickable.

Two things are better now. Skip ahead on the Briefing now waits for open
decisions ("Skip ahead is held for a waiting decision"), and "Negotiate a
window with the COO" now confirms the action with a clear toast.
