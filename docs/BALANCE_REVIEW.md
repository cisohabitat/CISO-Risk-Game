## Method

`pnpm --silent audit:balance 30` runs four declared strategies over the same 30 seeds in each of three modes: 360 campaigns. The baseline and revised engines use the identical script. Each strategy funds one programme, keeps a £350k discretionary reserve, commissions two enquiries, takes the first affordable option, clears affordable blockers, resumes only when staffing can meet demand, and attends the board. No policy uses future events or hidden risk rankings. Imposed cuts and emergency obligations can consume the reserve; it is a policy, not a protected account.

## Changes

- The existing 50% funding slider is now reachable when the full cost is unaffordable. The engine enforces the same floor; token allocations and unfunded resume bypasses are rejected. Full funding remains the default when affordable.
- Rejected starts, acceleration and blocker resolutions preserve resources. Acceleration requires the quoted quarter-cost funding. Partial funding records spending against the allocation, without inventing spending above it.
- Annual programme execution credits actual delivered progress. Starting at 0% earns no delivery credit; substantial unfinished delivery counts. Relevance, eight equal weights and the existing overall safeguards remain. Saved annual judgements are not rewritten.
- Existing identity enforcement and restore-window decisions become eligible from day 210, still requiring 80% programme progress. Their IDs, choices and consequences stay intact. This brings relevant follow-through into Q3; it does not insert filler or force a breach.
- The older tuning script now tries affordable alternatives and admits exact-budget programme purchases, avoiding false policy lapses.

## Limits and remaining validation

The longest decision-free interval remains about 69 days on CISO; the earlier follow-ups improve Q3 slightly, not the whole year. New results and programme delivery still stop Skip ahead. Human playtests must establish whether quiet periods feel purposeful, whether the reserve is understandable, and whether the outcome narrative explains trade-offs. No claim of complete human balance validation is made, and no blanket budget increase, objective easing or difficulty nerf was justified by these probes.
